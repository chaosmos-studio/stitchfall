Turn the pull. Find another path.

Stitchfall is a platform adventure made from cloth, thread and shifting gravity. Press Shift to turn the pull. Bridges change, timed seams open, and paths appear where there was only sky.

Collect sewn buttons, dodge needle moths, use spring flowers and find the zipper at the end of each room.

How to play

Keyboard: A/D or Left/Right to move, W or Space to jump, S or Down to crouch, Shift to turn gravity.

Phone: Slide to move, swipe up to jump, swipe down to crouch, and tap to turn gravity. The first Play opens an illustrated guide.
