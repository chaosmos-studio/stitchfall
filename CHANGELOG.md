## 1.1.1

- On a wall or the ceiling the controls turn with you: press or slide the way you want to go.
- Buttons you lose when you unravel now come back where they were.
- Home's menu sits in the middle of its pocket, and Play, Settings and the pause page's buttons fit their words.

## 1.1.0

- The room fills more of your screen, with the buttons beside it on wide phones.
- The first room teaches you to run, jump and turn the room, one move at a time.
- A calmer Home with one big Play button, and a Levels page with each chapter's patch and your next room marked.
- Simpler Settings, and How to play with bigger moves and a Practice run.

## 1.0.2

- Every pack in the Shop has its own page before you buy, with what it holds, its price and what the Complete pack saves.
- Tap a shut room to see which room opens it.
- Stuck in a room? A tag by the pause button shows where to skip it.
- The banner no longer shows on Settings or About, and Settings has a Shop button.

## 1.0.1

- Sign in with Google Play Games for twelve achievements, three leaderboards and progress that follows you to a new phone.
- Leaderboards and Achievements sit on the Stats page, in Settings.

## 1.0.0

- Stitchfall is here. Run, jump and turn the whole room over to sew your way through a world made of cloth.
- Every room plays a trick on you. Watch for the tell, laugh, then try again.
- Earn up to three stars a room, and dress your runner in the Wardrobe.
